About Us

Mentorship that goes beyond academics

Over the past five years, we have spearheaded mentorship initiatives across diverse grassroots communities, working closely with young people, community schools, local organizations, non-profits, institutions and the Government of Kenya. Driven by the vision of our founder, Brian Odhiambo, and a committed team, we deliver mentorship programs that create lasting change.

Our efforts include donating mentorship books to community schools and libraries, supporting girl-child empowerment initiatives, supporting bright but needy students, and providing guidance and counselling to young people.

In 2025 we launched the MBB Academy to address resource limitations. Funds raised through the Academy are reinvested to sustain and expand mentorship in schools and grassroots communities.

🎓 Mentorship with a Vice Chancellor

A one-on-one session with Prof. Isaac Ipara Odeo, Vice Chancellor of Kibabii University, featured founder Brian Odhiambo as mentee — nurturing his path toward authoring Wealth Within You.

🔬 STEM mentorship in Kisumu

Under the STEEP Project, MBB led STEM mentorship in community schools across Kisumu County, encouraging girls to pursue STEM subjects and careers.

How We Do It

Our Mentorship Models

🦅 The Eagle Mentorship Model

A high-impact, professional and practical approach that nurtures excellence among adolescents, students and young professionals. Programs are customised to individual and institutional needs, integrating professional speaking, personal branding, personal development, leadership growth and career readiness — through structured sessions, hands-on training and continuous guidance.

🔄 The Cascade Mentorship Model

Mentorship flows in a structured, progressive way so impact multiplies:

  1. Lead Mentor mentors Team Mentors
  2. Team Mentors mentor Young Professionals
  3. Young Professionals mentor Community Mentors

This builds leadership capacity, strengthens networks and brings guidance to more young people.

Our Impact in Action

From one campus to a global movement

MBB has grown from a single campus initiative into a mentorship movement transforming lives across Kenya, Africa and beyond.

  • 🎓 Reached over 100,000 students in schools across Kenya.
  • 💼 Trained over 10,000 young professionals from leading universities in confidence, communication and entrepreneurship.
  • 🌍 Expanded to youth in Nigeria, South Sudan, Uganda, Ghana, Zambia and more.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Inspired parents, teachers and communities to nurture potential beyond performance.
