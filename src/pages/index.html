Who we are

Mentorship that goes beyond academics

MBB is a youth-led global mentorship and empowerment community platform. For five years we have worked with young people, community schools, local organisations, non-profits, institutions and the Government of Kenya to deliver mentorship that creates lasting change.

We shape character, vision and resilience, guiding young people to discover who they are, why education matters and how to build a purposeful future.
