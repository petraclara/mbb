About the Academy

A structured learning platform for young people

MBB Academy equips young people with essential skills in communication, personal development and leadership. Participants take part in mentorship sessions, skills workshops and guided learning experiences that prepare them for education, career pathways and responsible leadership in their communities.

The Academy is also how we sustain our mission: funds raised are reinvested in mentorship activities in community schools and grassroots organisations, so vulnerable and underserved young people keep access to guidance, opportunity and hope.

Who should apply?

  • Students and young people ready to grow
  • Young professionals and emerging leaders
  • Aspiring mentors
  • Community-based organisations, school leaders and educators
  • Institutions interested in mentorship programs

Benefits

  • One-on-one mentorship from experienced MBB mentors
  • Access to professional networks and global mentors through MBB Mentors Connect
  • Practical skills and real-world knowledge
  • Learning resources, tools and expert guidance

Sustainable impact

Your growth funds someone else’s

Every Academy fee supports mentorship in community schools and grassroots organisations. When you invest in yourself with MBB, you also help a young person who might otherwise never have access to a mentor.

The Academy works closely with partners, mentors and community leaders to expand its reach and deepen its impact, building a future where every young person has access to guidance, opportunity and hope.
