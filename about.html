The MBB story

From one campus initiative to a global movement

Mentorship Beyond Boundaries began as a single campus initiative and has grown into a mentorship movement transforming lives across Kenya, Africa and beyond.

Over the past five years we have led mentorship initiatives in grassroots communities, working with young people, community schools, local organisations, non-profits, institutions and the Government of Kenya.

Our work has included donating mentorship books to community schools and libraries, supporting girl-child empowerment, supporting bright but needy students and providing guidance and counselling to young people.

Our founder

Brian Odhiambo

Founder, mentor, coach, author and community servant. Brian leads MBB with a commitment to help young people discover their purpose and turn it into opportunity.

His own mentorship journey includes a one-on-one session with Prof. Isaac Ipara Odeo, Vice Chancellor of Kibabii University, which nurtured his path toward authoring Wealth Within You.

Mentorship philosophy

How we mentor

We believe mentorship isn’t just a program, it’s a culture of growth, transformation and leadership.

🦅 The Eagle Mentorship Model

A high-impact, professional and practical approach that nurtures excellence among adolescents, students and young professionals. Programs are customised to individual and institutional needs, bringing together professional speaking, personal branding, personal development, leadership growth and career readiness through structured sessions, hands-on training and continuous guidance.

🔄 The Cascade Mentorship Model

Mentorship flows in a structured, progressive way so that knowledge, skills and values keep passing down, multiplying our reach into grassroots communities:

  1. Lead Mentor (Vision Carrier) mentors Team Mentors
  2. Team Mentors (Master Mentors) mentor Young Professional Mentors
  3. Young Professional Mentors / Volunteers (Emerging Mentors) mentor Community Mentors
  4. Community Mentors and Mentees (Foundation Level)
